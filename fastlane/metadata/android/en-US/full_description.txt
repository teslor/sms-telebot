SMS Telebot forwards phone events to Telegram bots, email addresses (via SMTP), or phone numbers (via SMS)

Stay informed when your phone receives:

- Incoming SMS
- Incoming calls
- Low battery alerts
- Charger connection status changes

Key Features:

- Flexible Routing: Create multiple rules to forward events to different destinations.
- Advanced Filtering: Powerful filtering by sender or message content with Regex support, whitelist and blacklist modes.
- Always-on background mode: Improved reliability on devices with aggressive battery/background restrictions.
- Automatic retries: Failed forwarding attempts are retried when temporary connectivity issues are resolved.
- Secure storage: Sensitive credentials (such as tokens, passwords etc.) are stored in encrypted form.
- Privacy-focused: No hidden servers, no trackers, no ads; data goes only to destinations you configure.

Important Notes:

- Battery Optimization: To ensure reliable performance, please exclude the app from battery optimization on your device.
- Manufacturer Restrictions: Some Android vendors apply strict background limits; behavior may vary depending on your device's firmware.
